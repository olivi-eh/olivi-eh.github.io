I get up from bed slightly shivering,<br>
And yet, isn't it still August?<br>
Sixteen degrees overnight and I'm pondering,<br>
These recent days haven't been the hottest.

A quick glance at my phone notifications,<br>
"Pumpkin Spice Lattes are back at Starbucks!"<br>
I suppose that based on these observations,<br>
Summer is over, and seasons are in flux.
